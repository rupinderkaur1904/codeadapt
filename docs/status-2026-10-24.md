## 6. Unresolved research risks

1. **No independent evidence of diagnostic accuracy.** Held-out cases (26 incorrect) were
   written by the error models' author; mutation audits are synthetic with behavioural labels.
   Under v1.3: held-out 14/26 named, 0 wrong; VAL1-A2 30/323 named, 0 wrong (synthetic).
2. **Low coverage.** Most incorrect programs get no named error; the failing-cases view is the
   main experience in practice.
3. **One algorithm family.** All 4 problems are binary-search variants with shared bug models;
   evidence across them is not independent.
4. **Skill maps and BKT parameters are unvalidated assumptions**; BKT is researcher-only.
5. **CodeNet licence/provenance**: reported CDLA-Permissive-2.0, not shipped with the data,
   no warranty on upstream rights, pseudonymous ids.
