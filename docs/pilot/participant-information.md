## CodeAdapt pilot: automatic feedback on C++ programming exercises

You are invited to try CodeAdapt, a research prototype that gives automatic feedback on
programming exercises. Please read this sheet before deciding. Taking part is entirely
voluntary.

**Who runs this study?**
- [Owner's name], an independent student researcher. This is not a course activity and
  has no supervisor.
- Contact: [contact details, given at Gate C].
- Ethics review: [name of the review body and reference, or "pending". The study does not
  start until it is granted].

**What is it for?**
- To find out whether the system works reliably for real learners and how clear and
  useful its feedback is.
- It is **not** a test of you, and nothing you do here is graded or shared with any
  teacher.

**What would I do?**
- Use the website in one or two sessions of up to 60 minutes. You solve short C++
  problems and read the feedback after each attempt.
- Afterwards, fill in a short questionnaire, which takes about 10 minutes.
- You can stop at any moment.

**Important: the feedback can be wrong.**
- CodeAdapt's error diagnosis and next-problem suggestions have been tested only on
  synthetic programs, not on real students.
- They may name the wrong mistake or miss one. Treat them as hints, not verdicts.
- The system makes no judgement about how much you know.

**Who can take part?** Adults (18 or older) learning C++ or algorithms. You cannot take
part if [exclusions from the protocol, for example a course the researcher grades].

**What data is collected?**
- A code name such as `p017`, never your name.
- Your password, stored only in a scrambled (hashed) form.
- The code you submit and when you submitted it.
- The output of your code on test inputs.
- The feedback the system gave you.
- Your device's IP address on login events. This is used for security only.
- Your questionnaire answers.

We do **not** collect your name, email address, grades or student ID in the system, and
there are no tracking cookies. Please do not type your name or other personal details into
your code.

**Who sees it?**
- Only the researcher. Every time the researcher views an individual's data, the system
  records it.
- The hosting provider runs the servers, [provider and region, Gate A].
- Your name is linked to your code name only on your consent form and in one encrypted
  file kept offline by the researcher.

**How long is it kept?**
- Research data is kept for at most 12 months after your last session, then deleted or
  irreversibly anonymised. [Final period set by the ethics review.]
- Security logs and backups are kept for shorter periods.

**Can I withdraw?**
- Yes, at any time and without giving a reason. Tell the researcher [contact]. Your
  account is closed at once, and your data is deleted within 14 days.
- Results that have already been combined into anonymous totals cannot be separated out
  again.

**Risks and benefits.**
- The risks are small: some frustration if the feedback is unclear or wrong, and the
  privacy risks described above, which are kept low by the measures listed.
- There is no direct benefit beyond practice with the exercises.
- No payment. [Review: token of thanks, if any.]

**Your rights.**
- Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, you can ask for information
  about your data, have it corrected or erased, and withdraw consent.
- If you have a complaint, contact the researcher first. If it is not resolved, you may
  approach the Data Protection Board of India.
- [Review: confirm the wording.]

**Results.**
- A summary of the findings, with no individual identifiable, will be available from the
  researcher on request.
- Reports will not claim that CodeAdapt improves learning or diagnoses errors
  accurately. This pilot cannot show that.
